# auth.md

## Agent access

JK.com is a public consulting website and blog. Agents may read publicly available content and follow the site's published links.

This site does not currently expose an OAuth authorization server or an automatic agent-registration endpoint. Non-public integrations require manual approval by the site owner through the contact flow.

## Provisioning

To request an integration or credentials, contact the site owner at https://jaysonknight.com. Include the intended agent, resource, requested access, and contact information.

Credentials, if issued after review, are scoped to the approved integration, must be kept confidential, and must be rotated or revoked when no longer needed.

## Supported methods

- Public, unauthenticated access to public website content.
- Manual provisioning for approved non-public integrations.

No unauthenticated account creation or credential issuance is performed by this site.
